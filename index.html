I became a soldier at just nineteen, and the journey was far from easy. Every day felt like a battle, not only against the physical demands of training but also against the doubts of those who believed I couldn’t endure this path as a young woman. I carried the weight of leaving my family behind, missing moments of comfort, and pushing through exhaustion when my body begged me to stop. The bruises, the sleepless nights, and the endless drills tested me, but they also shaped me. I had to prove my strength over and over, not just to others but to myself, reminding myself that independence means standing firm even when the world tries to shake you. My struggles became my armor, and though the path was harsh, it taught me resilience, courage, and the power of never giving up.
